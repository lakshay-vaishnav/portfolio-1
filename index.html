SchemeAI

AI • NLP • PWA • HACKATHON

AI-powered Indian government scheme discovery platform covering 73+ welfare programs with an offline rule-based bilingual engine.

Problem

Rural citizens miss government benefits due to portal fragmentation, language barriers, and spotty internet connectivity.

Thinking

Replaced unreliable, costly cloud AI APIs with a client-side rule-based NLP engine ensuring 100% uptime and 0ms latency.

Implementation

Token-based relevance scoring, education-level detection, Hindi/English bilingual parsing, PWA meta tags, and animated canvas branding.

Result

Indexed 73+ schemes with instant offline matching. Deployed to GitHub Pages as an official national Samsung challenge submission.
